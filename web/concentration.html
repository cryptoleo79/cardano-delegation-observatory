Delegation concentration

How concentrated voting weight is across the top-30 DReps — top-N share, the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI), and the Gini coefficient.

Top-N share of top-30 voting weight
Voting-weight distribution (top-30)

Descriptive, not a judgment. These indices report observed concentration across the tracked top-30 DReps (which hold the large majority of registered voting weight); they are not a network-wide measure and carry no "centralization risk" or "capture" claim — the reader interprets. HHI is the sum of squared percentage shares (0–10,000). Gini is 0 (equal) to 1 (concentrated). Source: api.asy.life/dreps · authority A. Related: Flows · Governance health.
